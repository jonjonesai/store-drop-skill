```
Returns & Refunds Policy

Last updated: {year}

At {brand_name}, we want you to love what you ordered. Since all of our products are made to order specifically for you, our return policy differs from traditional retail stores.

MADE-TO-ORDER PRODUCTS

All products sold by {brand_name} are printed and produced on demand after you place your order. Because each item is custom-made, we cannot accept returns for:
- Change of mind
- Incorrect size selection (please refer to our size guides before ordering)
- Color preferences (colors may vary slightly between screens and printed products)

WHEN WE WILL MAKE IT RIGHT

We stand behind the quality of our products. We will issue a replacement or full refund if:
- Your item arrives damaged or defective
- Your item has a printing error (wrong design, misaligned print, ink issues)
- You received the wrong item
- Your order was lost in transit

HAVE AN ISSUE? HERE'S WHAT TO DO

1. Contact us at {email} within 30 days of receiving your order
2. Include your order number and a clear photo of the issue
3. We will review your claim within 2 business days
4. If approved, we will either reprint and reship your item at no charge, or issue a full refund to your original payment method

REFUND PROCESSING

- Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days of approval
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method used at checkout
- Depending on your bank or credit card company, it may take an additional 5-10 business days for the refund to appear on your statement

ORDER CANCELLATION

Because production begins quickly after your order is placed:
- Orders can be cancelled within 2 hours of being placed
- After 2 hours, your order may already be in production and cannot be cancelled
- To cancel an order, contact us immediately at {email} with your order number

SHIPPING ISSUES

- If your order has not arrived within the estimated delivery window, contact us and we will investigate with our shipping carrier
- If your package is confirmed lost by the carrier, we will reship your order at no additional cost

EXCHANGES

We do not offer direct exchanges. If you need a different size or design:
1. Contact us about a refund for the qualifying item
2. Place a new order for the item you want

CONTACT US

For any questions about returns or refunds, reach out to us at {email}. We respond within 24 hours on business days.

We want you to be happy with your purchase. Something wrong? Tell us. We will make it right.
```
