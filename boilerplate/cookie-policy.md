Cookie Policy

Last updated: {year}

This Cookie Policy explains how {brand_name} ("we," "us," or "our") uses cookies and similar technologies on {domain} (the "Site").

WHAT ARE COOKIES?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and to provide information to website owners.

HOW WE USE COOKIES

We use the following types of cookies:

Essential Cookies
These cookies are necessary for the Site to function properly. They enable core features like shopping cart functionality, account authentication, and security. You cannot opt out of essential cookies.

Examples:
- Shopping cart contents
- Login session
- Security tokens

Analytics Cookies
These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our Site by collecting and reporting information anonymously. We use Google Analytics for this purpose.

Examples:
- Pages visited
- Time spent on site
- Traffic sources

Marketing Cookies
These cookies are used to deliver advertisements relevant to you. They may be set by us or by third-party advertising partners.

Examples:
- Social media pixels (Facebook, Instagram)
- Remarketing tags

THIRD-PARTY COOKIES

Some cookies are placed by third-party services that appear on our pages. We do not control these cookies. Third parties that may set cookies include:
- Google (Analytics, Ads)
- Facebook / Meta (Pixel)
- Stripe (Payment processing)
- PayPal (Payment processing)

MANAGING COOKIES

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can:
- View what cookies are stored on your device
- Delete some or all cookies
- Block cookies from being set
- Set your browser to notify you when a cookie is being placed

Please note that blocking or deleting cookies may impact your experience on our Site. Essential cookies are required for checkout and account functionality.

To learn more about managing cookies in your browser:
- Chrome: Settings > Privacy and Security > Cookies
- Firefox: Settings > Privacy & Security > Cookies
- Safari: Preferences > Privacy > Cookies
- Edge: Settings > Privacy > Cookies

CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the updated policy on this page.

CONTACT US

If you have questions about our use of cookies, please contact us at {email}.
